TLS / mTLS lab

Keys, certificates, extensions and Python SSLContext in one place. Change a control and the output updates.

Private keySecret. Signs and decrypts. Proves “I am me”.
Public keyDerived from the private key. Safe to share. Verifies signatures.
CSRPublic key + name, signed by the private key (proof of possession). The key is never sent.
Certificate (.crt)Public key + identity + dates, signed by a CA.
CATrusted signer. Everyone holds ca.crt. Only the signer uses ca.key.
mTLSBoth sides present certs and verify them against the CA.
ExtensionsRules inside a cert: is it a CA, what may the key do, which names are valid.
Proxy vs appUsually the proxy verifies; the app reads identity from trusted headers.

Rule of thumb: .crt is public and is what you present or trust. .key is secret and only signs locally.

    Extension file
    

    

    

    ExtensionWhy
    

    basicConstraintsSays CA or not. critical means a verifier that does not understand it must reject the cert.
    

    keyUsageWhat the key may do: digitalSignature for handshakes, keyCertSign for CAs, keyEncipherment only for RSA key exchange.
    

    extendedKeyUsageRole: serverAuth or clientAuth. Wrong role gives “unsupported certificate purpose”.
    

    subjectAltNameNames the client matches against. The CN is ignored.
    

    subjectKeyIdentifier / authorityKeyIdentifierFingerprint of this key / of the signing key. They link a cert to its issuer; strict mode requires them.
    

    

    Extensions come from the signer (-extfile), not from the CSR. Create the CA with its own SKI first, because the leaf AKI keyid copies it.
